Our story

The FinCoach Story

FinCoach began with a simple idea: finding the right loan shouldn't mean visiting branch after branch, filling in endless forms and guessing which offer is fair.

So we built one place to compare loans, deposits and cards side by side — with real rates, clear terms and a paperless application you can complete anytime, from anywhere.

For banks and FIs, FinCoach opens the door to a broad customer base at almost zero marketing cost — promoting financial inclusion and a more digitally empowered Bangladesh.
