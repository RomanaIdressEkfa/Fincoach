What do you need to apply for a loan in Bangladesh?

Documents, eligibility rules and practical tips to get your loan approved faster.

FC
FinCoach EditorialLoan & credit team
12 Aug 2026 5 min read

On this page

1. Check your eligibility 2. Prepare your documents 3. Compare before you apply 4. Keep your EMI affordable

Share

Applying for a loan is much easier when you know what lenders look for. Here's a simple checklist of documents, eligibility rules and tips to help you get approved faster.

Key takeaways

  • Most lenders want age 22–60, a stable income and a clean repayment record.
  • Keep NID, income proof and 6 months of statements ready.
  • Compare rates and fees side by side before you apply.

1. Check your eligibility first

Most banks and financial institutions in Bangladesh look at your age, income, job stability and repayment history. Typical requirements for a personal loan are:

  • Age between 22 and 60 years
  • At least 1 year in your current job (salaried) or 2 years in business
  • A minimum monthly income — often ৳25,000 or more

2. Prepare your documents

Keeping these ready can save days of back-and-forth:

NID & photos
Salary certificate / trade licence
6 months of bank statements
TIN certificate
Make sure the name on every document matches your NID exactly. Small spelling differences are one of the most common reasons for delays.

3. Compare before you apply

Interest rates, processing fees and early-settlement charges vary between lenders. Comparing several offers side by side — like you can on FinCoach — helps you see the real cost and pick the EMI that fits your budget.

4. Keep your EMI affordable

A common rule of thumb is to keep all your monthly loan payments below 40–50% of your income. Use the EMI calculator to test different amounts and tenures before applying.
